## Route-specific evidence for the 2-point item

- **A — Develop something new:** Explain the team's designed component, why it might help, and what happened when it was compared with a suitable reference under comparable conditions.
- **B — Kaggle:** Explain the design change and reason for it between **two meaningfully different pipelines**, each used for a valid submission. Compare local validation and Kaggle results for both, including any improvement or degradation and a useful error or leaderboard discrepancy.
- **C — Build a dataset:** Explain what the team collected or combined, consequential cleaning or quality checks, and how the resulting dataset fed an ML analysis. Show evidence about both data quality and what the ML result can or cannot tell the team.

## Scoring guide

Award the listed points when an item is clear and supported. Give partial credit when it is present but incomplete or hard to follow; give zero when it is absent or cannot be assessed. Half-points are allowed. Judge the quality of the investigation and explanation, not whether a method improved accuracy, a Kaggle rank was high, or a result was positive. A live demonstration is optional; a failed live demo can still earn evidence points when the team clearly presents saved results and explains what happened. Avoid counting the same weakness in multiple items unless it independently affects each one.
